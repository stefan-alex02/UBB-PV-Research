# UBB-PV-Research

## Title: The Impact of Data Representations on Enhancing Photovoltaic Energy Forecasting Performance

Abstract: The global necessity to decarbonize energy grids has accelerated the deployment of photovoltaic (PV) systems, yet the stochastic nature of solar irradiance presents persistent challenges for grid stability, unit commitment, and economic dispatch. Accurate forecasting is thus a critical prerequisite for reliable renewable integration. In this paper, we conduct a study on extracting meaningful embeddings from PV data for improving PV energy estimation, as well as an analysis on the impact of the temporal window of prior observations used for embedding generation. Two methods for embedding generation are comparatively evaluated using clustering methods and validated with supervised regressors. SHapley Additive Explanations is applied to assess the importance of features through the use of embeddings.
